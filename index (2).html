AccessPortland: TriMet ADA accessibility scoring tool

ArcPy, Python, Experience Builder | Spring 2026

  • Built a weighted ADA accessibility model for 3,418 TriMet transit stops across the Portland metro area.
  • Combined curb ramps, shelters, sidewalk connectivity and boarding conditions into one index that flags high-priority stops.
  • Published a public web app to explore scores by stop.

Spatial dendroclimatology of Netleaf Hackberry, Salmon River, Idaho

R, GLEAM v4.2a NetCDF | Spring 2026

  • Linked tree-ring chronologies at 14 Celtis reticulata sites to evapotranspiration and root-zone soil moisture.
  • Root-zone soil moisture was the strongest growth predictor, with August the critical month.
  • Won Outstanding Undergraduate Poster at the 2026 University of Idaho College of Science Student Research Expo.
