ArcPy, Python, ArcGIS Experience Builder

Spatial dendroclimatology of Netleaf Hackberry, Salmon River, Idaho

R, GLEAM v4.2a NetCDF

Spring 2026

  • Built an R pipeline linking tree-ring chronologies at 14 Celtis reticulata sites to evapotranspiration and root-zone soil moisture.
  • Used Pearson correlation, linear regression and seasonal windows. Root-zone soil moisture was the strongest growth predictor, with August the critical month.
  • Poster won Outstanding Undergraduate Poster at the 2026 University of Idaho College of Science Student Research Expo.
